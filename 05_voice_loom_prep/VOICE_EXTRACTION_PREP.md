## The Loom Itself · How to Record

**Setup:**
- Quiet room, decent natural light
- Webcam on (audio + video · we need cadence + body)
- 15-25 min, single take is fine, no editing
- No prep, no notes. The point is to catch her unrehearsed.

**Ask her to answer these 10 prompts, in order, in her own words. Don't read them out loud · paraphrase or riff. The more natural, the better.**

---

## The 10 Prompts

### 1. Origin Story (90 sec)
> Tell me how you ended up doing family business work. Not the resume version · the version you'd tell a friend at dinner.

*What we listen for:* the moment she got hooked, the first family that taught her something, what makes her actually care.

### 2. Who She Helps (60 sec)
> Describe the kind of person who books a call with you. Not demographic · what's going on in their head when they reach out.

*What we listen for:* the internal language her ICP uses. What they tell themselves. The fear or the frustration.

### 3. The Single Most Common Mistake (60 sec)
> What's the one thing you see family business owners get wrong over and over again?

*What we listen for:* her unique POV. The contrarian take. The thing she'd argue about.

### 4. A Story That Stuck (90-120 sec)
> Tell me one story · anonymized if needed · of a family business you worked with where something hard happened and you saw it through. What was the inflection point?

*What we listen for:* how she narrates. Detail vs abstraction. Where she gets emotional.

### 5. Why Sider Road (60 sec)
> What does it mean to you that Sider Road is itself a family business · that you work with Jade? What does that actually feel like day-to-day?

*What we listen for:* the authentic angle. The story we lean on in the EPK. Don't rehearse this with her in advance.

### 6. The Charisma Take (60 sec)
> You teach charisma. In one minute, what's the actual idea? Not the marketing description · the real one.

*What we listen for:* how she explains her own IP without slides. This becomes pitch shorthand.

### 7. Reputation by Design (60 sec)
> Without selling the book · what's the core idea of *Reputation by Design*?

*What we listen for:* the next book's positioning in her own voice. We need this for press pitches.

### 8. What Annoys Her (60 sec)
> What do you read or hear in the family business / leadership space that makes you roll your eyes?

*What we listen for:* what NOT to write. Anything she'd reject before reading. Cuts our rewrite cycles in half.

### 9. The Win She Wants (60 sec)
> Two years from now, what does success look like for Sider Road? Not bullet points · what's the picture in your head?

*What we listen for:* north star for strategy. Where she's aiming.

### 10. The Voice Check (45 sec)
> Read this sentence out loud, twice, in your normal voice: "Family business succession is rarely a money problem. It's a reputation problem dressed up as a money problem." Then say it again · but how YOU would actually say it.

*What we listen for:* her edit. We learn her sentence shape by hearing how she rewrites ours.

## What We Do With The Loom

1. Transcribe via Whisper (15-min job, runs on local machine)
2. Pull 30-50 verbatim phrases into a "Voice Bank" document
3. Annotate: humor signals, words she uses repeatedly, sentence length, where she goes hard, where she softens
4. Build a 1-pager "Jocelyn Voice Rules" sheet · what to do, what to avoid, sample sentences in her cadence
5. Every email, post, EPK section after this gets written using the Voice Bank + Rules sheet
6. Re-test by running one outreach email and one social post past her · if it sounds like her, we keep going
