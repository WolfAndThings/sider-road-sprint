## Pitch Order Rationale

Warm > cold every time. Sequence prioritizes reactivation and existing rapport:

1. **EPI first** · she already delivered there. Email Scott Snider / events team. Subject line: "Following up on charisma talk · open to 2026?"
2. **FBM Transitions Conference second** · runs in parallel with Transformational Women award nomination (separate deliverable). Two surface areas hit one event.
3. **FFI third** · academic + practitioner combo via Queens College adjunct title. Strongest credibility frame.
4. **NACD Tri-State chapter chairs** · local CT/NY proximity + paid honorarium standard.
5. **Vistage** · application-only but evergreen ROI once accepted.

After Tier 1, rotate Tier 2 + 3 in batches of 3-5 pitches per week.

## Warm-Flattery Tone (DO NOT DEVIATE)

Standard pattern per event email:

> [Last year's speaker or 2024 panel] was really sharp on [specific topic]. I work with [audience type] on [specific problem your signature talk solves] and would love to chat about being useful to [audience name] for 2026.

NOT: "I am a speaker available for hire." NOT: "Please consider me for your event." NEVER lead with hire-me energy.
