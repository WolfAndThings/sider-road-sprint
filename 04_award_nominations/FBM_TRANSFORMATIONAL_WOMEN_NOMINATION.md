## Suggested Nomination Statement (200-word version, copy-paste ready)

Jocelyn Greenky Underwood has spent more than 40 years guiding family-owned organizations through the leadership transitions that determine whether a family business survives a generation. She has personally walked more than 200 owners and successors through governance, succession, and reputation decisions, and shipped that hard-won pattern recognition as a 9-document operating system, four mastermind tracks, and a 15-course masterclass library that any next-generation operator can pick up and use.

Jocelyn is also her own case study. Sider Road LLC, the advisory practice she founded, is itself a family business: she runs it alongside her daughter Jade Herz. Few advisors in the field can credibly say they are doing what they teach. Jocelyn can.

Her path into this work is unusually broad: CEO at Colin Cowie Lifestyle and Ortho Marketing, Digital Global Editorial Director at Hachette Filipacchi, branded television producer at Rolling Stone's parent company. She brings operator scar tissue, not theory. In 2025 she joined Queens College CUNY as an Adjunct Professor of Business Administration. She is, by every meaningful measure, transforming the family business advisory field.
