Electronic Press Kit · structure + content

Bureaus, organizers, and warm referrals all ask the same questions in the same order. A real EPK self-serves those questions and converts inbound on autopilot. Two formats, both required, one published once.

Source · best-practice structure

Drawn from speaker-industry guidance via The Speaker Lab, SpeakerHub, Power Press Kits, and 120 Design Studio. Pattern is consistent across resources · one-sheet for cold pitch, full EPK page for warm review, both link to the same speaker reel and Calendly.

Format 01 · One-sheet (1-2 pages, PDF)

Cold-pitch deliverable. Attached to every event-organizer email, every podcast pitch, every warm-intro request. Front and back if needed, never more.

Required elements

  1. Headline · positioning statement, not "Speaker" · e.g. "40 years guiding family-owned businesses through the moments that decide whether they survive a generation."
  2. Professional photo · 1200×1200 minimum, recent, warm not stiff
  3. 4-5 signature speaking topics · title + 25-word abstract each
  4. Short bio · 100-150 words, narrative not resume
  5. Social proof · 2-3 strongest testimonials (concise, named, attributed) + client logos row
  6. Press logos · CNBC, Fox News, WSJ, Fast Company, HuffPost
  7. Contact · email + Calendly link + website
  8. QR code · links to full EPK page

Format 02 · Full EPK page · siderroad.com/epk or /press

Web page, not PDF. Shareable URL, mobile-responsive, easy to forward and screenshot. This is the page bureaus check after the cold pitch lands.

Required sections (in order)

  1. Hero · headshot + positioning headline + one-line subhead + "Book Jocelyn" CTA
  2. Long bio · 250-word narrative version (already drafted, see eSpeakers section above)
  3. Signature talks · 5 talks, each with title, 80-word abstract, ideal audience, takeaways
  4. Speaker reel · embedded video (60-90 sec, ships Week 2)
  5. Press section · CNBC, Fox, WSJ, Fast Company, HuffPost · each clickable to article
  6. Past speaking · 6-10 named past events (EPI 2025, NYU, Drexel, Columbia, etc) with date and topic
  7. Testimonials · 4-6 named testimonials with company + role
  8. Trusted by · 12 client logos in grayscale grid
  9. Fee transparency · "Keynote starting at $X · Half-day workshop $X · Full-day $X" OR "Inquire for current rates" if she prefers
  10. Downloads · One-sheet PDF · Long bio · Short bio · Hi-res headshot · Logo
  11. Contact / Book · dedicated speaking Calendly, separate from advisory

Why both formats matter

Bureaus and organizers consume content differently. Cold pitch · they want a 30-second skim. PDF attachment, easy to forward, hands them the answer. Warm review · they want detail. Web page, click to reel, click to articles, click to past events. Two formats, one consistent story.
