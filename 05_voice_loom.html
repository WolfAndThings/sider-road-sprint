Deliverable 05 · Voice Extraction Loom · Pending · your end

Twenty-five minutes that calibrate every email after this.

Every outreach email, every social post, every EPK paragraph after Week 1 needs to sound like Jocelyn. Not a polished version. The actual one. Word choices, sentence rhythm, where she lingers, where she gets sharp. The Loom is how we get there.

Owner Jocelyn (record) · Alex (process)
Length 15 to 25 minutes total
Format Single take Loom, webcam on

Why this step matters

Every deliverable downstream depends on this. Outreach emails, social posts, EPK rewrite, podcast pitches, pitch templates. They all need to sound like Jocelyn. Not "groovy older chick" as an abstract direction. The actual voice.

Without this, the work reads like a generic consultant agency wrote it. With it, every piece of copy after this point passes the "would Jocelyn say this out loud" test. That's the only test that matters.

The setup

  • Quiet room, decent natural light
  • Webcam on (audio + video, we need cadence and body)
  • 15 to 25 minutes, single take is fine, no editing
  • No prep, no notes. The point is to catch you unrehearsed.

The ten prompts

Ask Jocelyn to answer these in order, in her own words. Don't read the prompts out loud verbatim. Paraphrase or riff. The more natural, the better. If she gets stuck, skip and come back. Whatever she'd skip in real life is also a signal.

  1. Tell me how you ended up doing family business work. Not the resume version. The version you'd tell a friend at dinner.
    90 seconds
    Listening for · the moment you got hooked, the first family that taught you something.
  2. Describe the kind of person who books a call with you. Not demographic. What's going on in their head when they reach out.
    60 seconds
    Listening for · the internal language your ICP uses. The fear or the frustration.
  3. What's the one thing you see family business owners get wrong over and over again?
    60 seconds
    Listening for · your unique POV. The contrarian take. The thing you'd argue about.
  4. Tell me one story, anonymized if needed, of a family business you worked with where something hard happened and you saw it through. What was the inflection point?
    90 to 120 seconds
    Listening for · how you narrate. Detail vs abstraction. Where you get emotional.
  5. What does it mean to you that Sider Road is itself a family business, that you work with Jade? What does that actually feel like day to day?
    60 seconds
    Listening for · the authentic angle. The story we lean on in the EPK.
  6. You teach charisma. In one minute, what's the actual idea? Not the marketing description. The real one.
    60 seconds
    Listening for · how you explain your own IP without slides. Becomes pitch shorthand.
  7. Without selling the book, what's the core idea of Reputation by Design?
    60 seconds
    Listening for · the next book's positioning in your own voice. Needed for press pitches.
  8. What do you read or hear in the family business or leadership space that makes you roll your eyes?
    60 seconds
    Listening for · what NOT to write. Cuts our rewrite cycles in half.
  9. Two years from now, what does success look like for Sider Road? Not bullet points. What's the picture in your head?
    60 seconds
    Listening for · north star for strategy. Where you're aiming.
  10. Read this twice in your normal voice · "Family business succession is rarely a money problem. It's a reputation problem dressed up as a money problem." Then say it again, but how you would actually say it.
    45 seconds
    Listening for · your edit. We learn your sentence shape by hearing how you rewrite ours.

What we do with it

  1. Transcribe via Whisper inside 24 hours
  2. Pull 30 to 50 verbatim phrases into a "Voice Bank" document
  3. Annotate humor signals, words used repeatedly, sentence length, where she goes hard, where she softens
  4. Build a one-page "Jocelyn Voice Rules" sheet · what to do, what to avoid, sample sentences in her cadence
  5. Every email, post, EPK section after this gets written using the Voice Bank and Rules sheet
  6. Re-test by running one outreach email and one social post past her · if it sounds like her, we keep going

Optional · Tuesday May 19 working session

If the Loom leaves gaps, the 30-min Tuesday session becomes the calibration call. Focus on:

  • Specific opening lines for outreach emails (we read drafts, you rewrite on the fly)
  • Reviewing and approving the Voice Rules sheet
  • Final go-ahead on the EPK rewrite direction

The Loom is the foundation. Tuesday is the calibration. Both compound.
