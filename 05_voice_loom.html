Deliverable 05 · Voice Extraction · 35 questions · async

Thirty-five questions built to capture the real voice.

Replaces the original 60-minute live call with an async recording Jocelyn does at her own pace. Eight categories, 35 questions, designed to capture her voice, her background, her ethos, the moments that prove the work, and the lines that make event organizers want to book her on the spot.

Total 35 questions across 8 categories
Time Roughly 45 to 90 minutes total · split across sessions if needed
Format Audio for most, video for the speaker-credibility ones

How to record

  • Format: Loom, QuickTime, voice memo, or anything that captures clean audio. Video adds value for ~10 of the 35 (marked below) where on-camera presence matters.
  • Setup: Quiet room, good light if video, normal voice, no notes.
  • Pace: Don't try to do all 35 in one sitting. Three sessions of 12 to 15 minutes each works better than one 90-minute slog. Voice quality drops as you tire.
  • Skip: If a question doesn't land, skip it. The fact that you'd skip it is also a signal.
  • Send: Drop links or files in a Google Drive folder. We transcribe within 24 hours and pull verbatim phrases into the Voice Bank.

Format labels below: AUDIO = voice only is fine. VIDEO = on-camera adds credibility (use these for speaker reel + EPK + social).

Category 01 · 5 questions

Origin + background

How you got here. Not the resume version · the version you'd tell a friend at dinner.

  1. Tell me how you ended up doing family business work. What was the first family that taught you something you couldn't unlearn?AUDIO
  2. Walk me through your career arc · Philip Morris, Wenner Media, Hachette, Colin Cowie Lifestyle, Ortho Marketing, Sider Road. What pulled you from one to the next?AUDIO
  3. When did you first realize you had a real point of view on leadership and family businesses, not just experience?AUDIO
  4. What did your parents do, and how did that shape what you ended up doing?AUDIO
  5. If you weren't doing this, what would you be doing instead?AUDIO
Category 02 · 3 questions

Voice + cadence

Capturing how you actually sound when no one is watching · the rhythm we replicate in every email and post.

  1. Read this sentence out loud, twice, in your normal voice: "Family business succession is rarely a money problem. It's a reputation problem dressed up as a money problem." Then say it again, but how you would actually say it if you were telling a client.VIDEO
  2. What's a phrase or word you say constantly that other people notice?AUDIO
  3. When you're explaining something for the tenth time and it still doesn't land, what's your fallback move?AUDIO
Category 03 · 4 questions

ICP and audience

Who you actually serve, what's going on in their head, what they tell themselves.

  1. Describe the person who books a call with you. Not their demographic · what's going on in their head the week before they reach out?AUDIO
  2. What's the question every family business owner is asking themselves but won't say out loud?AUDIO
  3. When you're sitting across from a 65-year-old founder and a 35-year-old successor, what are they actually fighting about?AUDIO

Category 04 · 5 questions

Expertise and unique POV

The thing you actually know that almost no one else does. The contrarian take that makes someone stop and reconsider.

  1. What's the single most common mistake you see family business owners make over and over again?AUDIO
  2. In one minute, what's the actual idea behind Charisma is a Skill? Not the marketing description, the real one.VIDEO
  3. Without selling the book, what's the core idea of Reputation by Design?VIDEO
  4. What's the most overrated piece of leadership advice currently in circulation?AUDIO
  5. If you had to bet on which family businesses survive the next generation, what's your tell?AUDIO
Category 05 · 5 questions

Stories that prove it

Specific moments. Specific people. Real scar tissue. These become the anecdotes that get used in keynotes, podcasts, and the press.

  1. Tell me one story, anonymized if needed, of a family business you worked with where something hard happened and you saw it through. What was the inflection point?AUDIO
  2. Tell me about a client moment that surprised you · where you walked in expecting one thing and what you found was completely different.AUDIO
  3. When was the last time you watched someone get charisma exactly right? Walk me through what they did.AUDIO
  4. Tell me about a reputation event you watched up close · either a save or a derail · and what you learned from it.AUDIO
  5. What's a moment from your CEO days at Colin Cowie or Ortho Marketing that you keep going back to mentally?AUDIO
Category 06 · 5 questions

Ethos + beliefs

What you stand for, what you reject, the lines you won't cross. The values stack that makes everything else cohere.

  1. What do you read or hear in the family business or leadership space that makes you roll your eyes?AUDIO
  2. What's a line you won't cross with a client? Where does the work stop?AUDIO
  3. What do you believe about leadership that most of your peers would disagree with?AUDIO
  4. If charisma is a skill, what's the thing it can't fix?AUDIO
  5. Two years from now, what does success look like for Sider Road? Not bullet points · the picture in your head.AUDIO
Category 07 · 4 questions

Why hire you as a speaker

The pitches that make event organizers stand up. Sound bites for the EPK, sizzle reel, and bureau profiles.

  1. In 60 seconds, why should an event organizer book you over any other family business speaker on the bureau roster?VIDEO
  2. What's the one thing audiences come up to you about after a talk? What do they remember?VIDEO
  3. What's the best talk you've ever given? Where, what was the topic, what made it land?AUDIO
  4. If you had 30 seconds on stage and could only leave the audience with one idea, what's the idea?VIDEO
Category 08 · 4 questions

Sider Road as a family business itself

The unique credibility angle that no other advisor in the space has. Lock the authentic narrative.

  1. What does it mean to you that Sider Road is itself a family business · that you work with Jade? What does that actually feel like day-to-day?VIDEO
  2. What does Jade bring to Sider Road that you couldn't bring alone?AUDIO
  3. When you walk into a family business client meeting and they realize you run Sider Road with your daughter, what shifts?AUDIO
  4. In 20 years, what do you want Sider Road to be?VIDEO

What we do with the 35 answers

  1. Transcribe within 24 hours of receipt (Whisper, runs locally)
  2. Voice Bank · pull 50 to 75 verbatim phrases into a single document, tagged by category
  3. Voice Rules sheet · 1-pager covering humor signals, sentence shape, repeated phrases, what to avoid, sample sentences in her cadence
  4. EPK rewrite · bio + signature talk descriptions + interview questions get a voice pass against the Voice Bank
  5. Sizzle reel script · pull on-camera lines from category 2, 4, 7, 8 questions for the speaker reel narrative spine
  6. Social caption library · 30 to 50 caption starters drawn directly from her answers, ready for the content engine
  7. Pitch personalization · subject lines and opening lines lifted from her actual voice into every email template

Optional · Jade's 3-prompt mini-session

While Jocelyn records her 35, Jade records 3 prompts in Paris. Vertical iPhone, natural light, single take per prompt, 30 to 60 seconds each:

  1. What it's like working with your mom at Sider Road.
  2. Why someone should consider working with your mom.
  3. One thing you've learned at Sider Road that you didn't expect.

Raw clips go in the same Google Drive folder. We catalog them for the content engine work post-sprint.
