06 · Why This Order

Sequencing logic.

Each move comes before the next for a specific reason. Skip the order, you skip the leverage.

01

Sprint 1 (Surface) BEFORE outreach lands

Bureaus, event organizers, and journalists ALL check your site within 90 seconds of getting an email from you. If the EPK isn't ready, the press logos aren't surfaced, the talks aren't titled, they say no. Sprint 1 is the gate.

02

Door A (GHL) BEFORE Door B (Social)

Fastest cash · plumbing exists, list is warm, 1-3 weeks to first replies. Door B compounds over months. If you can only afford one, start where the money is closest.

03

Revenue moves BEFORE positioning moves

Branded framework, Sider Road Institute, Cornell Fellow application · these tier-jump moves work BEST once paid bookings are on the board to leverage them against. Doing them first is positioning theater. Doing them after Sprint 2 produces is leverage.

04

Sprint 3 ONLY after Sprint 2 produces

Earned trigger · 2+ booked engagements OR 5+ qualified inbound replies. You don't hire an ongoing engine for nothing. Sprint 3 kicks in once there's actually a system to maintain.

05

Book launch + Institute brand container = Month 5+

By Month 5 you'll have bureau wins, GHL replies, LinkedIn inbound, AND a forthcoming book. That's the moment to launch "Sider Road Institute" + the named method · maximum credibility surface, real distribution.
