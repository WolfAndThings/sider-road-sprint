What this is + why it matters

Sprint 1 packaged what you already have · EPK, bureau outreach, awards, event pitches, site fixes. That's surface work. Sprint 2 is the engine that converts the surface into revenue. Sprint 1 makes you bookable. Sprint 2 makes you booked.

The 8 moves

  1. GHL list reactivation. You have years of email contacts sitting cold in GHL. We build a 5-email sequenced re-engagement campaign that brings them back with a warm hook (new book, new mastermind cohort, new framework). Expect 3-8% of a dormant list to re-engage. On a list your size, that's real money.
  2. LinkedIn outreach playbook for M. M sends 30 LinkedIn connection + DM touches per day to a curated list of ideal prospects (family business owners, estate planning attorneys, wealth advisors). You approve every message before it goes. Steady drip, no spam, fills your calendar.
  3. Masterclass funnel. Right now your free + paid offerings sit on the site but there's no bridge between them. We build a funnel: free download → email nurture sequence → low-ticket course offer ($547 Charisma) → mastermind application. Each step warms the prospect for the next.
  4. Mastermind fill campaign. Three of your four masterminds aren't full. We run targeted ad + outreach campaigns per cohort: Successors (next-gen family biz leaders) · Authority Women (senior women execs) · Founders (private-company CEOs). Each campaign tuned to where its ICP hangs out.
  5. Lawyer + advisor partnership outreach. Estate planning attorneys and wealth advisors all have clients who need family business succession help · and most don't have a name to refer to. We build a referral pipeline by reaching out to 50-100 of them with a partnership offer. One good referral partner = 2-3 engagements per year.
  6. Branded signature framework. Right now you have great talks but no named "Jocelyn Greenky method." Tier-above family business speakers all have one (the Davis Three-Circle Model, etc). We name your framework and put it on the site so journalists, bureaus, and event organizers have a single phrase to attach to your work.
  7. Sider Road Institute. Add an "Institute" container to your brand (Sider Road Institute for Family Business). This is the move that signals you're an institution, not a sole proprietor · and it's how speakers get to the $25K+ tier. Costs nothing structurally · just framing + a section on the site.
  8. Cornell SC Johnson Fellow application. Cornell runs a Family Business Fellow program. Being a Fellow adds an Ivy-league affiliation to your bio and opens speaking + research doors. We draft the application packet so it's submission-ready.

How we'd run it

Per project or retainer · whatever fits your budget + timeline. We can scope each of the 8 as standalone or bundle them as a 4-week build. Sprint 3 = ongoing retainer if Sprint 2 produces.
