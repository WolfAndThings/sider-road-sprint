Signature talks

Five talks, each available as keynote (45 to 60 min), workshop (half-day or full-day), or fireside conversation. Tailored to audience.

Talk 01 · Most-booked

Charisma is a Skill

The science of professional presence. Charisma is not personality, it is a learnable repertoire of behaviors: how you enter a room, how you listen, how you take up space, how you make others feel central. Across six interlocking moves, Jocelyn shows audiences how to operate with the kind of magnetism that opens doors, closes deals, and earns trust before tenure does it for you.

Best for: CEO peer groups, women's leadership conferences, sales kickoffs, board meetings.

Takeaway: A 6-step Charisma audit attendees use the next day.

Talk 02 · Forthcoming book hook

Reputation by Design

Reputation is the silent currency of leadership. It opens doors before you walk through them and protects you when you make mistakes. This talk maps the deliberate decisions that build a reputation that compounds for decades, drawing on Jocelyn's forthcoming book of the same name and 40 years inside the rooms where reputations are made and broken.

Best for: Executive women, professional services firms, board governance forums, family business gatherings.

Takeaway: A reputation audit framework and the five reputation traps that derail careers.

Talk 03 · Family business core

Family Leadership & Legacy

Most family businesses die at the transition between generations, not because of business problems but because of family decisions made under business pressure. Jocelyn shares the playbook from 200 family business transitions: how to design governance that holds, how to have the conversations no one wants to have, and how to build a legacy that survives the founder.

Best for: Family business conferences, wealth manager forums, multi-generational ownership groups.

Takeaway: The 9-document Family Business Playbook framework, free to attendees.

Talk 04 · Cross-generational

Generational Fluency

Four working generations now sit in the same boardrooms, mastermind rooms, and family business kitchens. The leaders who thrive are not the ones who pick a side. They are the ones who become fluent across all four. Jocelyn unpacks what each generation actually wants, what they are misreading about each other, and how a leader builds a culture that lands across all of them.

Best for: HR conferences, leadership development summits, family business succession events.

Takeaway: Generational fluency map plus three communication rules per generation.

Talk 05 · Practical edge

Office Politics for Honest People

You can be honest and effective inside a political organization. You just need a framework. This talk gives the audience a working strategy for being taken seriously without playing dirty, drawn from four decades of executive coaching inside Hachette, Wenner Media, Colin Cowie Lifestyle, and dozens of family-owned businesses.

Best for: Women's leadership conferences, professional women's groups, mid-career executive audiences.

Takeaway: The five political archetypes you will meet and the one move that disarms each.
