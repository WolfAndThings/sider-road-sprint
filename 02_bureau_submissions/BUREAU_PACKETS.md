## What Bureaus Will Look At First

1. Speaker reel (60-90 sec sizzle, audience cutaways, on-stage clarity)
2. Headshot quality
3. Press logos
4. Fee range (too low = bargain bin, too high = no demand)
5. Topic clarity (one obvious answer to "who books this person and for what")

The reel is the missing piece. Week 2 builds it from existing webinar footage. Without it, listings are 60% strength.

---

## Risk / Caveat

Bureau acceptance rates are low. Plan for both bureaus to take 4-12 weeks, and assume the direct-pitch event database (sibling deliverable) is the actual revenue lever. Bureaus are credibility surface + long-term inbound, not Q1-Q2 cash.
